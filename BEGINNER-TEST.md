# Beginner test: 20 minutes, 3 to 5 people

Pick people who have thought about running but have not started, or started and stopped. Not runners.

Rules for you: hand over the phone, say nothing, do not explain. Watch where they pause or look confused. Note exact words.

Open https://runchennai.vercel.app and ask them to do these one at a time:
1. "You want to start running next week. Show me what you would do first."
2. "Find a place near you where you could run."
3. "Find a group you could join. What worries you about joining?"
4. "Is there anything you were hoping to find that is not here?"
5. "Would you send this to a friend who wants to start? Why or why not?"

Write down for each person:
- Where did they get stuck or leave?
- What question did they ask that the site did not answer?
- Which words confused them?
- Did they feel "I can do this" or "this is not for me"?

Send the notes to Claude. Fix the top 3 problems before adding anything new.
